Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data this Discord bot ("the Bot", "Carry Ticket Bot") collects, why it's collected, how it's stored, and how you can request its removal. By using the Bot in a server, you agree to the practices described below.

1. What We Collect

  • Discord user ID and username — to identify who opened a ticket and who is interacting with it.
  • Discord role IDs — to check ticket-access permissions (e.g. blacklist roles, staff roles) via the Bot's access control settings.
  • Message content within ticket channels — messages sent inside a support ticket channel are read and saved into a transcript when the ticket is closed.
  • Attachment metadata — filenames and URLs of files/images shared in a ticket (the files themselves remain hosted on Discord's CDN; we do not download or re-host them).
  • Timestamps — when tickets are created, messages sent, and tickets closed.

We do not collect data from channels outside of ticket channels, and we do not read messages in channels where a ticket is not active.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To generate a ticket transcript so staff and the ticket creator have a record of what was discussed.
  • To enforce role-based access control, such as preventing specific roles from opening tickets or specific ticket categories.
  • To display ticket history and status on our web dashboard for staff to review.
  • To apply cooldowns that prevent ticket spam.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data described above is stored on our own server infrastructure (not a Discord-provided service), separately from Discord. This includes:

  • Ticket transcripts (HTML files containing message content, usernames, and timestamps)
  • Configuration data (role IDs, user IDs) used for access control
  • Ticket metadata (creation/close timestamps, ticket counters, cooldowns)
Transcript links shared with ticket creators expire after a limited time (currently 1 hour) for security, but the underlying transcript file remains stored so staff can access ticket history through the dashboard.

4. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use message content or any collected data to train machine learning or AI models.
  • We do not track presence/online status.
  • We do not collect data from servers or channels where the Bot is not actively managing a ticket.

5. Retention & Deletion

Ticket transcripts and related metadata are retained to preserve support history. If you would like your data removed (for example, a specific ticket transcript deleted), contact the server administrators who operate this Bot, or reach out using the contact method below.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request a copy of data associated with your Discord user ID.
  • You may request deletion of your ticket transcripts and associated data.
  • You may leave any server using this Bot at any time, which stops any further data collection from that server.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

For questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, please contact the server administrators operating this Bot, or reach out via Discord.
