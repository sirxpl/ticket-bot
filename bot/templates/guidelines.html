📜 Carry Service System Rules

Guidelines for the Carry Service & the point system that governs it

This section dictates transactions and assistance protocols specifically for Roblox Tower Defense Simulator (TDS). All #「📚」rules within the premises of the server will still be followed with mandatory procedures.

For Clients (Users receiving help)

Respect the Team

Treat carry operators with absolute respect. Toxicity or elitism results in ticket closure and disciplinary action.

Refrain from Wasting Time and Effort

Idling, being unready, or stalling a carry ticket on purpose will result in your ticket being dissolved and your account being flagged.

No Duplicate or Troll Requests

Do not open tickets for game modes you have already beaten, or open joke/troll tickets. The only exception is mission quests.

Do Not Spam The Staff nor The Carry Service Members

Do not ping carry members in public channels or DMs asking for speed. Wait your turn in the queue.

Follow Directives

Follow the tactical commands and strategies of the carry lead during a match. Going rogue allows the team to abandon the run and close your ticket alongside existing server penalties.

Ticket Throttling (Cooldowns)

Standard clients may open exactly one (1) ticket every 8 hours after ticket closes. Specialized trial queues are limited to one (1) ticket every 2 hours.

For Carry Team Members (Operators)

Maintain respect for all clients regardless of their skill level

Mocking mistakes or showing toxic elitism results in instant role revocation.

Patience with Various Timezones

Clients are global. If a client is temporarily offline due to scheduling/latency, leave the ticket open and pivot to help another active client instead of deleting the request.

No Abuse of Power

Closing active tickets without a valid, documented reason or adding unauthorized third parties to private ticket rooms will result in immediate removal from the team.

Strict Confidentiality

Internal team strategies, application details, management logs, and private staff conversations must never be shared in public chat channels, tickets, or external platforms.

Mandatory Activity

Carry members must maintain a visible presence. If real-world schedules force an extended absence, you must formally notify the Administration beforehand, or you will be quietly decommissioned for inactivity.

Points Info

Points are earned per client based on the game mode, the difficulty variant, and how many carriers were on the run. Fewer carriers on a run earns more points per carrier.
