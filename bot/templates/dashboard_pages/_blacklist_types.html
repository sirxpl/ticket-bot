Blacklist Roles

Add the roles available to blacklist commands and ticket checks.

▾

Add a name, Discord role, and default reason for each restriction. Select that role in /addticketblacklist. No Regular or Voidcore presets are added. For category restrictions, also add the same role to each affected category's blacklist roles in Carry Manager Settings. The bot needs Manage Roles and must be above the role.

Removing a field removes that choice for future commands. Existing member restrictions keep their original role and scope until removed or expired.
