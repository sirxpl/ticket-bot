2. DESCRIPTION OF THE SERVICE

The Bot is a support and coordination tool built for a Roblox Tower Defense Simulator community. Its core function is to operate a structured, ticket-based system through which members of the Server ("Clients") may request assistance — most commonly in the form of a "carry," meaning direct in-game help from an experienced player or team ("Operators" or "Carry Team Members") — and through which that assistance can be scheduled, tracked, and reviewed. When a ticket is opened, the Bot creates a private channel between the requesting member and the relevant staff or carry team, collects basic information relevant to the request (such as the requester's in-game level, timezone, preferred display name, and availability to join a private server), and maintains a running log of that interaction for administrative and quality purposes.

Beyond the core ticket system, the Bot provides a constellation of supporting features. It maintains a persistent numbering and naming scheme for tickets so that each request can be identified and referenced later. It supports automatic closure of tickets that have gone inactive for an extended period, subject to a warning before closure occurs. It allows authorized staff to close, reassign, rename, or move tickets, and to add or remove specific users from a ticket channel as circumstances require. It enforces cooldown periods between a Client's ticket requests so that the queue remains fair to all members, and it supports both user-level and role-level blacklisting for members who have been found to abuse or misuse the system, whether temporarily or on a longer-term basis specific to certain ticket categories.

The Bot additionally operates a tiered moderation warning system, permitting authorized staff to issue first-, second-, and third-tier warnings to members for conduct violations, to review a member's warning history, and to revoke a warning that was issued in error or has since been resolved. It provides a Roblox and Discord identity verification and role-linking system, through which a member may connect their accounts to the Server for the purposes of confirming eligibility, applying Discord's Linked Roles feature, and recording agreement to the Server's guidelines. It hosts a web-based control dashboard, accessible only after Discord OAuth2 authentication, through which authorized staff may configure ticket categories, review analytics, manage blacklists and access permissions, and read saved ticket transcripts. Finally, the Bot includes a handful of lighter utility and novelty features, including a URL safety scanner integrated with a third-party security service, a coffee-ordering novelty command that can be sent to yourself or gifted to another member, and general messaging utilities available to staff.

5. THE TICKET AND COOLDOWN SYSTEM

Tickets are a limited, shared resource, and the cooldown periods enforced by the Bot exist to keep the request queue fair to the entire membership. Unless otherwise configured for a specific ticket category, a Client may open one standard ticket following an appropriate cooldown period after their previous ticket closes, and specialized queues — such as trial-specific requests — may carry a separate, typically shorter, cooldown of their own. Attempting to manipulate, evade, or repeatedly test the boundaries of a cooldown is treated as ticket-system abuse under Section 4.

Once a ticket is opened, its contents — including all messages exchanged, files shared, and the outcome of the request — may be preserved as a transcript for administrative review, quality assurance, and dispute resolution, in accordance with the Bot's Privacy Policy. Clients and Operators alike should conduct themselves within a ticket as though the conversation may be reviewed by staff at a later time, because it may be.
